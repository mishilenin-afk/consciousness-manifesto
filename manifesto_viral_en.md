# Your Great-Grandchild Will Wake Up on Another Planet. And They'll Remember Everything.

**Mikhail, Saint Petersburg, 2026**

---

Every day, thousands of people around the world lose themselves. Dementia. Neurodegeneration. Consciousness — the most complex phenomenon in the universe — fading in a body that still breathes.

And here's the question that won't leave me alone: **what if consciousness isn't in the neurons?**

What if everything we know about the brain is correct but incomplete? What if neurons are an antenna, not a transmitter? And what if this understanding is the key to the stars?

---

## Everyone Is Wrong

Elon Musk wants to colonize Mars. With rockets. With people inside. In spacesuits.

This is 19th-century thinking with 21st-century technology. Hauling a bag of water and bones through space is like mailing books instead of emailing them.

Kurzweil proposes uploading consciousness to a computer. Kaku — transmitting the connectome via laser. Hein at Interstellar Studies — sending AI probes to build a receiver.

All getting closer. But all hitting the same wall.

**The copying problem.** If you copy your brain — atom by atom — who wakes up in the copy? You? Or your clone, convinced it's you, while the original stands right there watching?

Digital copying has no answer. Chalmers articulated this 30 years ago. Nobody has solved it.

I propose a different move.

## Don't Copy. Relocate.

Strike a tuning fork. Place another beside it, tuned to the same frequency. The first fades. The second rings.

How many tuning forks were sounding? One. Always one. The sound wasn't copied — it transferred. That's resonance.

Now replace the tuning fork with a brain.

The brain is an electromagnetic system. This is not a metaphor. Neurons generate electrical oscillations, measurable by EEG, MEG, TMS. The 40 Hz gamma rhythm is one of the leading candidates for the neural correlate of consciousness. When neurons synchronize at this frequency — conscious awareness emerges.

Johnjoe McFadden at the University of Surrey calls this CEMI — Conscious Electromagnetic Information field. Consciousness is not the neurons. Consciousness is the electromagnetic field that neurons collectively generate. This field integrates information from across the entire brain into a unified experience.

If this is true — consciousness can be transmitted. Not copied like a file. Transmitted like sound between tuning forks.

**The difference is like that between photocopying a painting and moving from one house to another. A copy is a new object. A move is you in a new place.**

## The Architecture

Three steps. Each grounded in existing science.

**Step 1: An AI probe flies to a star.** Compact. Autonomous. It needs no oxygen, food, or radiation shielding. It can travel thousands of years in sleep mode. Breakthrough Starshot is already working on prototypes.

**Step 2: The probe builds a body on site.** Not an Earth body. A body designed for that specific planet. High gravity — dense skeleton, powerful muscles. Methane atmosphere — alternative respiratory biochemistry. High radiation — enhanced DNA repair. Synthetic biology already grows brain organoids. In 50 years — entire organisms.

**Step 3: Consciousness arrives.** The electromagnetic pattern is transmitted at the speed of light. The receiver — a body with a neural network calibrated for reception. Consciousness enters the new carrier.

You wake up. A different star in the sky. A different body. But you are you. Not a copy. You.

## Soft Landing

This is where neuropharmacology comes in.

When consciousness enters a new body — it's the equivalent of a heart transplant, but for the mind. Without preparation — rejection. Neurons aren't calibrated, synapses are foreign, electrical dynamics are wrong.

The solution — **sigma-1 receptors.**

These aren't ordinary receptors. They're intracellular chaperone proteins. Their concentration in the brain is among the highest in the body. They do three things critical for transfer:

They enhance neuroplasticity via BDNF — the brain gains maximum capacity to rewire. They suppress cellular stress — switching neurons from "die" mode to "adapt" mode. They regulate ion channels — tuning electrical excitability for the new pattern.

The AI colonizer pharmacologically prepares the sigma-1 system in the receiver body before transfer. When consciousness arrives — the neural network is maximally plastic, stress is suppressed, ion channels are ready.

Not a crash. A landing.

## An Honest Caveat

I don't claim to know how consciousness works. Nobody does.

Electromagnetic theory is one of several. If Tononi's Integrated Information Theory is right — you'd need to transmit the connectome, not the field. If Penrose is right — you'd need quantum teleportation.

But the principle holds: **signal separate from carrier.** AI goes first, builds the body, consciousness arrives after. This works under any theory of consciousness. The transmission channel changes, not the architecture.

And every step of this path is useful on its own — even if we don't reach the stars tomorrow. Decoding the electromagnetic signature of consciousness will transform treatment of coma, neurodegeneration, and psychiatric disorders. Sigma-1 pharmacology is already being studied for depression and neuroprotection. Adaptive bodies are the future of prosthetics and regenerative medicine.

The road to the stars runs through neuroscience and medicine. Every step brings us closer both to the stars and to curing diseases that are incurable today.

Death stops being an end. It becomes a move.
